# Uber Trips Analysis — Key Insights & Business Recommendations

## 1. Overall Trip Performance
The dataset contains approximately 50,000 trips. Of these, 42,540 were completed (85.08%), 4,984 were cancelled (9.97%), and 2,476 were marked as No-Show (4.95%).

**Recommendation:** Investigate cancellation and No-Show patterns by city, time slot, and driver to identify potential operational improvements.

## 2. City Performance
Boston recorded the highest trip volume (approximately 8.5K trips) and the highest reported actual revenue (approximately $115K). Revenue across the six cities was relatively similar.

**Recommendation:** Evaluate driver availability, estimated arrival time, and cancellation rates in Boston before increasing driver capacity. Investigate the reported lost-revenue figures to confirm their definition and calculation.

## 3. Driver Performance
Driver performance should be evaluated using completed trips, revenue, cancellation rate, and customer ratings where available.

**Recommendation:** Recognize consistently high-performing drivers through performance-based incentives. Investigate unusually high cancellation rates relative to each driver's total trips before taking corrective action.

## 4. Time-Slot Performance
Late Night and Midday recorded the highest trip volumes, at approximately 12.6K trips each. These periods also generated the highest reported revenue.

**Recommendation:** Review driver availability, waiting times, and unserved demand during these periods. Adjust driver schedules only when the data confirms a coverage gap.

## 5. Day-of-Week Performance
Saturday appears to have lower trip volume than other days.

**Recommendation:** Validate this pattern across a sufficiently long period and across cities. If the decline is consistent, consider adjusting driver shifts while maintaining adequate service coverage.

## 6. Customer Loyalty
Frequent customers may represent an opportunity to improve retention.

**Recommendation:** Test a limited loyalty or rewards program and measure incremental completed trips, retention, and profit after rewards costs.

## 7. Cancellation and No-Show Reduction
Cancelled trips represent 9.97% of recorded trips, while No-Show trips represent 4.95%.

**Recommendation:** Capture cancellation reasons, identify the responsible party, and examine ETA and timing patterns. Test fair, transparent policies for repeated confirmed No-Show cases rather than applying automatic penalties without further investigation.

## Conclusion
The analysis highlights opportunities to improve trip completion, driver scheduling, customer retention, and operational efficiency. Recommendations should be validated against additional operational data before implementation.
